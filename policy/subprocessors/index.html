Subprocessors

The companies that handle personal data for Sonde, what each one does, and what it receives.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

A subprocessor is a company that handles personal data on Sonde's behalf so we can run Sonde and this site. We use as few as we can and send each one only what its job needs.

Companies that handle personal data for Sonde

Company What it does for Sonde What it receives Privacy information
Google Cloud Runs Sonde's hosted service in the United States, and Sonde's company email, including legal@sonde.tech. The source code and websites you ask Sonde to analyze, including names and email addresses in commit history; account and license records; service logs; email sent to a sonde.tech address. Google Cloud privacy notice
Cloudflare Delivers this site and Sonde's report downloads, checks the contact form with Turnstile, and measures visits with Web Analytics when analytics is allowed. Each visitor's IP address and request details; with analytics allowed, cookieless page-view measurements; for a report download, the recipient's email address and the report, kept for up to 30 days. Cloudflare privacy policy
WorkOS Signs customers in to Sonde. Name, email address and sign-in details, including the profile of a Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub account used to sign in. WorkOS privacy policy
Stripe Takes payments and handles sales tax as the seller of record. Billing name, email address and address, and payment details. Stripe privacy policy
Keygen Issues and checks Sonde licenses. License keys and the customer and organization each one belongs to. Keygen privacy policy
HubSpot Keeps Sonde's customer and prospect records, including contact-form requests. What you enter in the contact form, and the name, email address and company of customers and early-access testers. HubSpot privacy policy
Twilio SendGrid Sends Sonde's email notifications, such as contact-form alerts to staff and report download links. The recipient's email address and the message, which for a contact-form alert includes what you entered. Twilio privacy notice
GitHub Hosts this site's files through GitHub Pages. Request details, including the IP address of the service that fetches each file. GitHub privacy statement

Changes to this list

We give 30 days' notice on this page before a new company starts handling personal data for Sonde. To ask about or object to a change, write to legal@sonde.tech.
